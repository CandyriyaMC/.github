# Governance

CandyriyaMC is run as a community-first open-source organization. This document explains who decides what, so contributors know how decisions happen.

## Roles

- **Lead maintainer** — Radmir ([@bejiihiu](https://github.com/bejiihiu)). Final say on direction, releases, repo creation and org settings.
- **Maintainers** — the [Maintainers](https://github.com/orgs/CandyriyaMC/teams/maintainers) team. Review and merge PRs, triage issues, cut releases, share decisions with the lead.
- **Contributors** — anyone sending PRs, issues, documentation or help in discussions.

## Decision making

- Day-to-day technical decisions are made by lazy consensus among maintainers: propose in an issue or PR, allow a reasonable comment window, proceed if there's no sustained objection.
- Contested or wide-impact decisions (new repos, licensing, releases, security policy) are decided by the lead maintainer, informed by maintainer discussion.
- Maintainer permissions are granted at the lead's discretion, based on a track record of quality contributions and constructive review behavior. There is no fixed contribution count — judgment over metrics.

## Changing this document

Propose changes via PR to this file. Governance changes are approved by the lead maintainer.

## Enforcement

[Code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) violations are handled by the lead maintainer (warning → temporary → permanent ban), proportionate to severity. Reports go to bejiihiu@icloud.com.
